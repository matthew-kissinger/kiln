## Findings to retain

- References work in normal iteration: the first completed Muse A/C duct runs
  exported an exact one-line `serviceOffset` change without resending the asset.
- Progressive discovery needs grouped lookup. Muse C used nineteen signature/
  capability calls before building; the baseline catalog needed one lookup.
  This measures a call-budget tradeoff, not a visual-quality advantage.
- Opaque execution errors can undo source-transfer savings. Muse B retained a
  failed revision but then sent two small probe programs and a rewritten full
  asset after receiving only “Generated asset execution was rejected.”
- Budget awareness is unreliable without measured counters. Muse B claimed
  twenty-four calls remained after consuming most of its allowance. The host
  refused calls 31 and 32; the revised animation inspection was incomplete.
- Keep export and inspection outcomes separate. A valid final source/GLB pair
  does not imply the model completed the requested attachment/motion review.

## Harness setup failures

The first two Astra duct authoring phases produced artifacts, but their follow-up
phases could not use tools because of dispatcher configuration. They are
ineligible for full-journey comparison and will not be relabelled as model or
engine failures.

`--approve-for-me` belongs to `exec`, before the `resume` subcommand. Placing it
after `resume` fails argument parsing before a model request. Removing the flag
causes headless `exec` to apply its default approval policy and reject MCP calls.
The retained correction uses `codex exec --approve-for-me resume ...` and keeps
the original deadline. No model-response retry was performed. Failed command
invocations and permission responses remain in the evidence.

## Interpretation limits

One attempt per cell is exploratory. Two setup failures, call-budget exhaustion,
and possible geometry defects preclude a clean aggregate ranking. Review the
actual source differences, attachment views and animation frames separately.
MCP image blocks and OpenCode PNG attachments establish harness delivery; model
self-reports are retained separately from independent image-consumption evidence.

The new grouped-discovery and authoring-error refinements were prompted by these
observations. They must be tested in fresh final journeys; changing this frozen
cohort halfway through would confound the comparison.

## Fixed-model cross-harness availability

A read-only authentication inventory found no second configured subscription route
for the same provider/model. OpenCode lists its own stored credential; its OpenAI
and Google options are API-key routes, outside this run's subscription-only scope.
Hermes reports `openai-codex` and `google-antigravity` logged out. No credentials
were copied and no additional model call was made. The cross-harness comparison
is therefore unavailable under the current setup. Comparing Muse/OpenCode with
Astra/Codex would confound model and harness.

no formal human ratings have been collected, so there are no quality scores.
