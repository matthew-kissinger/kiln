# Variable duct

Build a compact industrial air duct, 2.4 m long. It transitions from a circular 0.7 m intake to a rounded rectangular 0.9 × 0.45 m outlet along a curved centreline. The passage is open at both ends with visible wall thickness; include exterior reinforcing ribs and a detachable inspection cover. Keep the changing section smooth enough to read without hiding the transition under many boxes.

ServiceAssembly is the inspection cover and its mounting bracket. serviceOffset controls its position along the duct; moving it must carry its fasteners and bracket together.

Define an animation named `ServiceMotion` lasting one second. The inspection assembly slides 0.2 m away from its closed position and returns; sample at 0, 0.5 and 1 seconds.

Use metres. Keep all geometry in the retained JavaScript program, name the inspection assembly exactly `ServiceAssembly`, and expose a numeric constant named `serviceOffset`. Do not import an existing asset or copy a finished example. Render and review the result using the tools available. Save a first source revision before any follow-up edit.

Review criteria: Assess open passage, section transition, curved centreline, wall thickness and coherent cover attachments.
