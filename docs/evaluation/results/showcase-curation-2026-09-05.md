# New showcase curation

The maintainer reviewed actual exported images and regenerated six completed
Gemini/Muse candidates with the same `gallery-studio-v1` camera recipe and square
framing as the collection. Source geometry and materials were not hand-edited.

| Asset | Decision | Visible basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brass Tellurion | Accept, supporting example | Clear brass/blue instrument silhouette; illustrative gears and stylized land patches remain explicit limitations. |
| Ribbon Tea Pavilion | Accept, supporting example | Readable curved roof, woven underside and coherent open seating area. |
| Solar Sail Courier | Accept | Distinctive faceted gold sails, dark trusses and compact central body; no claim of physical deployment fidelity. |
| Seed Vault | Accept, supporting example | Coherent open shell and specimen carousel; white surfaces remain bright. |
| Kinetic Wave | Accept | Clean teal/bronze fin rhythm, readable pivots and dark plinth; no certified mechanical-clearance claim. |
| Alpine Cable Terminal | Accept, supporting example | Coherent timber module and exposed pulley. Pale cabin glazing and ending cables remain visible; one visual refinement completed before the call budget. |
| Deco Radio | Accept, supporting example | Grille and controls read clearly after orientation repairs; tuning markings remain pale. |
| Polar Rover | Accept | Readable tracked orange vehicle with attached instruments and solar panels. White roof highlights remain bright. |
| Orchid Conservatory | Hold out | Green/brass frame reads well, but pale glazing mutes the floral interior; adds little over the existing greenhouse. |
| Clockwork Manta | Reject | Correct square framing fixes clipping, but detached-looking wingtip rods and broken-looking attachments remain visible. |
| Iris Lantern | Hold out | Closed shutters obscure the glass and interior; the vertical-shutter design misses the requested radial iris. Maintainer also opened the chosen final PNG. |

The accepted poster receipts and unchanged source hashes are checked in beside
each example. [The Muse report](showcase-muse-0d457e8f-2026-09-05.md) additionally
records reviewer rejection of Canyon Funicular and holding out Glasswing Drone.
Those decisions concern visual quality, separately from successful export.

The two Astra additions have their own [review and export comparison](astra-showcase-review-2026-09-05.md).
