# Gallery pixel review: alphabetical assets 18–35

Root inspected each actual 1024-square PNG in `examples/renders` with `view_image`
after the shared gallery refresh on 5 September 2026. This reviews presentation,
not hidden topology, mechanical operation or fabrication suitability.

All 18 have consistent neutral backgrounds, complete silhouettes and visible edge
margins. None needs a framing correction to close the gallery-consistency request.
Material brightness varies with the authored materials. The pinball playfield is
hard to read through its reflective cover; it is a possible future curation target,
not a new rendering-correctness claim or a recommended hero.
