## Visual review

- **Solar Sail Courier:** distinctive folded gold foil and exposed truss spars. A strong silhouette
  and useful custom-mesh example. Its ivory core is bright in the tool contact sheet.
- **Seed Vault:** coherent open pod shell, copper ribs, green interior, connected drawers and a
  specimen carousel. A quieter architectural study. Its white surfaces need the common gallery
  lighting to preserve shape.
- **Kinetic Wave:** the clearest restrained design in this batch. Curved bronze and teal fins form
  a deliberate progression on a dark plinth. Its final part-detail camera is too close for a poster;
  the whole-asset view is suitable.
- **Deco Radio:** a useful visual-repair example. The first draft had obscured dial/grille faces and
  protruding decorative bars. Revisions opened the frames, corrected depth ordering and fitted the
  rear panels. The final dial remains pale and has ticks rather than numeric labels.
- **Polar Rover:** a readable orange tracked expedition vehicle with instruments, solar panels and
  a mast. The roof is bright in tool lighting. Its rear-detail shot is too close to serve as a
  presentation image; use the whole-asset view.

The root review subsequently accepted all five using actual square gallery posters. The contact
sheets retained here are authoring evidence, not the gallery thumbnails. Long contact-sheet labels
clip in some cells. No source was changed by the evaluator to make a run appear successful.

## Failures retained

The first Solar Sail bootstrap failed before authoring because the local GPU service had stopped.
Its observer recorded zero calls and images. The owned process tree was stopped; after the root
restarted the service and confirmed health, a new workspace and conversation began. A health check
preceded every later launch. There was no CPU fallback, quota retry loop or paid API substitution.

Seed Vault's second substantial edit introduced an undeclared material variable. A third exact edit
repaired it. Polar Rover's original draft failed evaluation; the model repaired geometry parameters,
then later corrected another failed equipment revision. Deco Radio also recovered from a harness
file-writing argument error. These are recovered failures, not first-pass successes.

GPU responses reported full-material fidelity on the local RTX 3070/D3D12 renderer. Material fidelity
does not establish collision-free mechanics or watertight geometry. Export hashes refer to the
installed Node package's artifacts; later gallery rebuilds have their own hashes and provenance.
